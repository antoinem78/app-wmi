Founder correction, 2026-08-10, and he was rightly furious: "We have already done and sent the store first look". I had twice called Vincent's InstaWarm store first-look overdue, on the sole basis that no copy of it existed in `docs/`. It had been written and sent to the client on time, over Slack, a surface I cannot read.

**Why it mattered more than a normal slip:** I did not just get it wrong in chat, I relayed it to Bernard as fact ("appears still outstanding"). He accepted fault for an overdue deliverable that was never overdue, filed gate status against that false premise, and built a four-item blocking list whose first item was fiction. Correcting an agent's memory afterwards costs more than checking would have, and it briefly made a diligent agent look negligent.

**How to apply:** client-facing deliverables are sent over Slack, email and Upwork. The repo is where I keep drafts, not a record of what went out. So the absence of a document in the repo says nothing about whether it was delivered. Ask the founder, or say "I cannot see whether this was sent" and leave it as a question. Never write a delivery status into a relay, a memory, or `PROJECT_STATE.md` unless the founder confirmed it or a system of record shows it.

Same family as [[meta-api-absence-claims]] (never assert absence from one reading) and [[check-access-before-requesting-it]] (read the live system, not the repo). The founder's standing ruling covers this: check the surface a real user sees before any "there is no X" claim. A grep is not that surface.
