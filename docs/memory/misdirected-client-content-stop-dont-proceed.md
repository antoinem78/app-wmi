Founder correction, 2026-09-01, after it happened twice in one Steffen Foerster session: **"when you see a mistake like this don't act on it."**

Both times a message arrived in the Steffen channel carrying GoPoxy material. First a paragraph about attracting distributors, which is GoPoxy's B2B track and has no counterpart in a one-photographer workshop business; he replied "stop!!!!!!!! wrong channel". Later, GoPoxy grout creatives and an instruction to update Ryan, GoPoxy's contact, mixed in with two genuine Steffen tasks.

**The second time I flagged it and proceeded anyway**, opening with words to the effect of "I will do all of it, but flag that at the end rather than hold anything up". Flagging was right. Continuing was not, and having already been corrected once made it worse.

**Why it matters:** a channel session holds one client's standing rulings, history and constraints and none of the next client's. GoPoxy in particular is freelancer-managed and **analysis-only**, with recommendations routed through the founder rather than acted on, and a different session owns `docs/clients/gopoxy.md`. So work done from the wrong channel can contradict the session that owns it, breach a ruling this session cannot see, and write to a file it does not own. Channel ownership is the whole mechanism keeping parallel sessions from colliding, per PROJECT_STATE §7.

**How to apply:** when a message contains material that clearly belongs to another client, **stop and name what looks misdirected, then wait.** Do not split the message and action the parts that fit this client. Do not offer to do the rest anyway, because offering is a form of proceeding and it puts the founder in the position of having to decline. Being busy is a reason to route work correctly, not a reason to absorb it here. Sibling of [[no-cross-client-comparisons]]: that one governs what goes into a client's analysis, this one governs what to do with input that arrived in the wrong place.
