Ruled 2026-08-18. The founder keeps his human contractors: Gopoxy and Steffen are freelancer-managed and that is the model, not a transition state. Bernard (Meta) and Oscar (Google) hold build and optimise capabilities, but they run **only when the founder triggers a session** (audit first, then build and/or optimise), to complement, improve or correct the freelancer's work. **Norbert, a separate model, supervises both agents**: his review precedes the founder's on every build or optimise session.

**Why:** an agent writing to an account a human also manages is a race unless writes happen in founder-opened windows. The earlier Gopoxy "watch-and-flag only" ruling (2026-08-13) is the resting posture, not a contradiction: between founder-triggered sessions, agents observe and flag.

**How to apply:**
1. Never propose standing/scheduled agent writes on a freelancer-managed account; on-demand founder dispatch is the only write path.
2. Pre-flight on such accounts reads the platform's own change history, not just our `move_snapshots`, so human changes count toward the thrash gate.
3. An agent move that would reverse a recent human change must say so in the approval item; the founder arbitrates between agent and freelancer, never the agent.
4. Client-facing and freelancer-facing writing never frames the agents as replacing the contractor.
