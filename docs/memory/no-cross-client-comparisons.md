In the GoPoxy day-one baseline I described their Meta misconfiguration as "the Brunos disease" and their healthy funnel as "in sharp contrast to Brunos." The founder's correction: "please don't compare this with Brunos. Brunos is a shitty account with no sales. This is a proper business."

**Why:** comparing a real business to a failing account misframes the client, and if the framing leaks into client-facing writing or shapes strategy, it insults a paying client and cross-contaminates confidential account knowledge between clients. A shared technical pattern does not need the other client's name: platform behaviour explains it on its own (e.g. "Advantage+ treats inclusions as suggestions; only exclusions are hard limits").

**How to apply:** findings stand on the account's own data plus platform behaviour, never on another client's account. Benchmarks, if needed, are industry norms or the account's own history. Other clients' names do not appear in any per-client analysis, internal or client-facing. Related discipline: [[client-document-voice]], [[cross-surface-relevance-check]].
