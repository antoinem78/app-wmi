**Antek operates entirely under Baptiste's identity.** Baptiste's Slack account, and Baptiste's Google Ads, GTM, GA4 and Cookiebot accounts. **Antek's name is never visible to Kyle or any other client.** Confirmed by the founder 2026-08-20.

So: a Slack message the founder pastes into a session labelled "from Antek" appeared to the client as **Baptiste**. Internal notes addressed to Antek alone can use his name. **Anything entering a client-visible thread must say Baptiste.**

**Why this is written down:** on 2026-08-20 I asserted in VIP's channel file that Kyle already knew of Antek, on the reasoning that "he replied to Kyle directly under his own name". That was wrong. I had read the founder's own attribution in a pasted transcript as though it were the Slack presentation. A drafted reply to a thread Kyle reads named Antek five times and was caught only because the founder corrected me. Nothing was sent.

**How to apply.** A pasted transcript tells you who *wrote* something, not what the recipient *saw*; the founder adds attributions for your benefit. Never infer a person's visibility to a client from it, and never build an argument on that inference. Before any name goes into client-facing copy, confirm which identity that person operates under. Kyle addressing a message to "Anthony & Baptiste" was the available clue and it was there to be read.
