**What Cowork found, and it moved the review's spine.** Store is `superhenry.com`, backend `aphym5-e9.myshopify.com`, admin `super-henry-2`. Window 11 July to 9 August: 647 sessions, 16 orders, 2.16% conversion, total sales GBP 468 on GBP 624 gross less GBP 156 discounts, all Online Store channel. **The reason is arithmetic, not demand, and it is now the lead finding.** One-time is GBP 29.25 and pre-selected by default; Subscribe and Save is GBP 25.50 for the first order then **GBP 34.00 every four weeks**, so from order two onward subscribing costs more per month than simply buying one-time at the promo price. A rational parent picks one-time every time, and 24 of 24 orders confirm they do. Appstle Subscriptions is installed and working; the incentive is simply inverted. Fixing it is a pricing decision on Rhodri's side, not a rebuild.
