# Dupe-exclusion audience (Denis 5a): design note and dry-run record

**2026-09-03, platform session.** From the Denis dossier addition of 2026-08-13, adopted into the Code brief: a nightly job that hashes every owned CRM contact into one customer-list exclusion audience per Meta ad account, excluded from every managed ad set, so Meta is never paid to re-acquire a contact we already hold. The brief ordered dry-run first (plan only, zero writes), then per-client go-live watching CPM drift, and said custom-audience writes are founder-by-hand or fix-path territory. This note records the dry run as built and the rulings go-live needs.

## What is live

`MAINT_dupe_exclusion_plan` (n8n `PFwbFdUKQ1uxeprU`, generated by `scripts/build-dupe-exclusion-workflow.py`), nightly at 03:15 UTC plus a `Run now` webhook behind the dispatch key. Per Meta ad account it:

1. Reads every contact from each tenant's GoHighLevel location that shares the account (today: DentalMastery and WMI, both on `act_1027063116856202`), paging by 100.
2. Normalises and SHA-256 hashes email and phone to Meta's customer-file spec, deduplicates across tenants, and counts. The hashes exist only inside the run; nothing but counts is logged or posted.
3. Reads the account's custom audiences and ad sets with their targeting.
4. Writes the plan to `action_log` (workflow `MAINT_dupe_exclusion_plan`, status `skipped` because nothing was written) and posts a summary to #alerts: does the audience `WMI | Owned contacts (exclusion)` exist, how many matchable rows, which live ad sets lack the exclusion, and the exact writes go-live would perform.

Zero writes to Meta or GoHighLevel, by construction: the workflow has no POST to either.

## Go-live: three rulings before any write

1. **Who creates the audience.** Two options: the founder creates `WMI | Owned contacts (exclusion)` by hand in Ads Manager once per account and pastes the id into the client config, or a governed fix-path workflow creates it. Recommendation: by hand, once. It is five minutes and it keeps audience creation outside the agents' write grammar.
2. **The upload write.** Sending hashed rows to `/<audience_id>/users` is a new Meta write class, not covered by the optimise grammar. It needs either its own governed leg (nightly, replace mode, read back the audience's approximate count, fail closed on a size drop) or a founder-by-hand CSV upload each time. Recommendation: a governed leg, because "each time" is nightly and by-hand will not happen nightly.
3. **The exclusion itself needs no new machinery.** Excluding the audience from each ad set is exactly BERNARD_optimise's `audience_exclude` move: Norbert reviews, the founder approves per move, the verifier diffs the read-back. Five moves on this account today, under the daily ceiling of three, so two sessions.

## Compliance flag, for the founder and the DentalMastery workspace

Uploading hashed CRM contacts to Meta is a transfer of personal data to a processor for advertising suppression. For WMI (UK, PECR and UK GDPR) and DentalMastery (ADENERGY, Poland, GDPR) the privacy notices need to cover it, and Meta's customer-list terms require it. Nothing has been uploaded, so nothing is due yet, but ruling 2 above should not be taken before the notices are checked.

## Dry-run guarantees

- Read-only credentials paths only: GoHighLevel private integration tokens and the Meta header credential, on GET requests.
- Hashes never persist: the Plan node discards them after counting.
- Error handler set; a read failure posts the plan as incomplete rather than silently as zero.
