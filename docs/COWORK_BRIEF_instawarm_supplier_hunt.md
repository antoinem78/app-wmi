# COWORK BRIEF: InstaWarm, find a heated jacket that ships with a power bank

**Date:** 2026-09-03
**Requested by:** Anthony
**Type:** Research. **Read and report only. No purchases, no accounts, no contact with anyone.**

## The job in one line

Find heated jackets sold by dropshipping or wholesale suppliers that **include a power bank in the box**, and that look like the jacket the store already sells.

## Why

The store owner sells a USB heated jacket at $149.95. It ships without a battery, so a customer waits two to four weeks and then cannot switch it on until they buy something else. He wants to include one. His current supplier does not bundle, and a listing he found on Eprolo turned out not to bundle either despite being chosen for that reason. **The whole point of this search is that one line in a listing: does a power bank come in the box.**

## Rules, and they are firm

1. **Do not buy anything.** Do not click Add to Cart, Buy Now, Add to Import List, Import to Store, Order, or Request Sample.
2. **Do not create an account on any platform, and do not sign in to anything.** If a site hides product detail behind a login, report exactly what you could see without one and mark the rest "login required". That is a useful finding, not a failure.
3. **Do not contact any supplier.** No chat widgets, no enquiry forms, no "message seller", no quote requests.
4. **Quote verbatim.** Every claim in your report must be the supplier's own words in quotation marks, with the URL. Paraphrase is worthless here, because the entire history of this project is suppliers whose wording did not survive contact with reality.
5. **If something is not stated, write "not stated".** Never infer a specification from a photo, a title, or another listing. A gap you name is useful; a gap you fill is a liability.
6. **Do not judge quality, safety or reliability.** You are collecting evidence, not recommending a supplier. Say what the listing claims, not whether it is true.

---

## What the jacket has to be

The visual match matters more than any specification, because the owner has over $1,000 of video content shot on his current jacket and will not switch to something that looks different. Compare against `https://instawarm.shop/products/heated-jacket`.

**The target garment:** black hooded puffer, horizontal channel quilting in even bands across chest, back and sleeves, rounded hood with a centre back seam and no drawstring, a single square-housed control button with a circular power icon on the wearer's left chest, centre front zip, two zip pockets at the hip.

## Scoring criteria

For every candidate, answer all ten with verbatim evidence or "not stated".

**Must have. A candidate failing any of 1 to 6 is not a candidate, but still report it and say which one it failed.**

1. **Power bank included in the box.** The listing must say so in words. A battery appearing in a photo is not evidence, and neither is a title containing "with battery" if the description contradicts it. Quote the packing list or box contents exactly.
2. **Battery specification.** Capacity in mAh and output in volts and amps. 10,000mAh and 5V/2A or better is the target.
3. **Visual match** to the garment described above.
4. **Sizes S to 4XL at minimum.** S to 6XL preferred. Report the exact size range, and note if the range differs between variants of the same listing, which has caught us before.
5. **Ships to the United States**, with the stated delivery window and, if given, the warehouse or country it ships from.
6. **Cost.** Unit price plus shipping, so the landed cost per jacket. Under $60 keeps the retail price viable.

**Preferred, and worth reporting either way:**

7. **Does it heat the arms?** Quote the coverage claim and describe any zone diagram. This one is worth flagging loudly if yes, because it would fix a problem in the store's existing advertising rather than creating a new one.
8. **Heat settings.** All temperatures and their indicator colours.
9. **Size chart**, complete, with units and any note on how it is measured.
10. **Water wording** exactly as written, and any CE, FCC, RoHS or UN38.3 documentation mentioned.

## Where to look, in this order

**1. AliExpress.** Fully public, no login needed, and the largest catalogue. Spend the most time here.

**2. Alibaba.** Mostly public. Different proposition, since sellers there will bundle on request for a minimum order, so note any listing whose description mentions custom packaging or an OEM option even if the standard listing has no battery.

**3. CJ Dropshipping.** Public catalogue at cjdropshipping.com, partially visible without login. Has US warehouses, which would cut delivery times substantially. Report what is visible and mark the rest login required.

**4. Zendrop and 5. Spocket.** Both likely to be behind a login wall. Spend little time and report the wall rather than working around it.

**Search terms worth trying:** "heated jacket with power bank included", "heated jacket battery included", "USB heated jacket set with battery", "heated jacket kit", "heated jacket with battery pack". The differentiator is always the word "included".

---

## Output

**A decision document, not a list of links. Maximum six candidates.** Better to return three well-evidenced ones than ten thin ones.

For each candidate: the platform, the URL, a one-line description, then all ten criteria with verbatim quotes or "not stated", then a plain verdict of qualifies or fails, naming the criterion it failed.

Then a comparison table of the qualifying candidates against the six must-haves, so the choice is visible at a glance.

Then, and this matters as much as the candidates:

**Your assessment of whether any of this is achievable.** If you sweep these platforms and find that essentially nobody bundles a power bank with a heated jacket, say so plainly and give your evidence for it: roughly how many listings you looked at, and how many said in words that a battery is included. **That is a genuinely valuable answer, not a failed search.** It would convert an open question into a settled fact and send the owner down a different route. Do not pad the report with near-misses to avoid returning that result.

Finally: **anything you noticed that the owner would end up repeating on his website and might not be able to stand behind.** Waterproof claims, temperature claims, certification claims, battery runtime claims. Quote them.

**Estimated time: 90 minutes.** Stop at that point and report what you have, rather than continuing until the list is full.
