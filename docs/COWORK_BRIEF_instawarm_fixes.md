# PART A: the edits

## Step 1. SHIPPING page, delivery times. 12 minutes

**Content → Pages → SHIPPING**

This is the most important edit in the brief. The page promises USA delivery in 5 to 10 business days. The owner's real figure is 10 to 20, and his FAQ says 15 to 21. Three claims, and the fastest one is the one buyers read.

**1a. Find this paragraph:**

> Our goal is to offer you the best shipping options, no matter where you live. Every day, we deliver to hundreds of customers across the world, ensuring that we provide the very highest levels of responsiveness to you at all times.

**Replace with:**

> Shipping is free, worldwide. Every order is checked and packed before it leaves, which takes 3 to 5 days, and then it is tracked the whole way to you. The table below is the honest delivery window for each country, not the best case. If yours is running late, email me and I will chase it.

**1b. Replace the entire shipping time table.** It currently reads USA 5-10, Australia 5-8, Canada 5-10, Europe 5-10, Worldwide 8-15 business days. Replace with:

| Country | Dispatch | Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| USA | 3 to 5 days | 10 to 20 business days |
| Canada | 3 to 5 days | 12 to 25 business days |
| Australia | 3 to 5 days | 10 to 20 business days |
| Europe | 3 to 5 days | 10 to 20 business days |
| Rest of world | 3 to 5 days | 15 to 30 business days |

Keep the existing "Processing time" explanation above it if it is still accurate at 3 to 5 days. Save, then screenshot the published page on the storefront, not the editor.
