# COWORK BRIEF: InstaWarm, read a new supplier's product page

**Date:** 2026-08-28
**Requested by:** Anthony
**Type:** Read and report only. **No edits anywhere, on any system.**

**The page:** `https://eprolo.com/app/mproductdetail.html?id=18310305`

Eprolo is a dropshipping platform. The page renders when signed in. It is a single-page app, so the product data appears only after the page loads properly.

**Why this matters.** The store owner is switching to this supplier so he can bundle a power bank with the jacket. He says he does not expect many changes to his website. In the last three weeks, five separate things on that site turned out to describe a different product from the one he actually ships: the battery inclusion, the heating zone count, the size chart, the review photos and whether it heats the arms. Every one of those came from a supplier page or a supplier's word and every one looked like a small detail beforehand. **So the job here is to find the differences, not to confirm the similarities.**

---

## Rules

1. **Read only. Change nothing.** Do not edit, save or submit anything on any site.
2. **Do not click "Add to Import List", "Add to Cart", "Import to store", "Order" or anything that adds this product to an account or store.** This is a dropshipping platform and those buttons have real consequences.
3. **Do not place an order, do not contact the supplier, and do not use any chat or enquiry form.**
4. **Transcribe verbatim.** Where the page states a figure or a claim, quote it exactly rather than summarising. Paraphrase is not useful here: the entire point is catching wording differences.
5. **If a field is not stated on the page, say "not stated" rather than inferring it.** A gap you name is useful. A gap filled with a guess is worse than nothing.

---

## What to report

### 1. Heating zones

How many, and where are they? **Specifically: does this jacket heat the arms or sleeves?** Quote whatever the page says, and describe any heat-map diagram image showing where the panels sit.

### 2. Temperature settings

All three settings, with their temperatures and the indicator colour for each. Quote exactly, including units.

### 3. Size chart

The **complete** chart, every row and column, with the column headings exactly as written and the units. Also transcribe any note about how the measurements are taken or how the sizing compares to US or European sizing.

### 4. What is in the box

The packing list or package contents, verbatim. **Confirm whether a power bank is included, and if so its capacity in mAh and its output in volts and amps.** This is the whole reason for the supplier change, so be precise.

### 5. Shipping

Delivery time to the United States, processing or dispatch time, and **where it ships from**. Eprolo operates local warehouses as well as shipping from China, so note which applies. Quote any figures exactly.

### 6. Price and variants

Cost per unit, any quantity breaks, and the list of variants: colours, sizes, and any other options.

### 7. Waterproofing and materials

The exact wording used about water resistance. The listing title says "Waterproof". Report precisely what the body of the page claims, since "waterproof" and "water resistant" are different promises. Also report the shell and lining materials, and any washing instructions.

### 8. The images

Describe every product image. For each, note whether it shows the jacket alone or with other items, whether any text or callouts are burned into the image, and what those say.
